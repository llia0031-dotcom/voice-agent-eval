# Disfluent speech and real dialogue data: saved excerpts (fetched 2026-11-14)

## LARD (github.com/tatianapassali/artificial-disfluency-generation, 15 stars, no licence detected by GitHub, last push 2022-09-28; paper LREC 2022, arXiv 2201.05041)

> You can use the LARD tool to auto-generate disfluencies such as repetitions, restarts, and replacements.

The tool works on English sentences (contextual embeddings over English text); its three types are repetitions, replacements and restarts.

## HumDial Challenge (ICASSP 2026; https://aslp-lab.github.io/HumDial-Challenge/)

From the challenge home page and the Track 2 (full-duplex) page:

> Teams can register by the google form
> Please use your institutional or corporate email address to register, and avoid using personal email accounts.
> The data will be sent via the registered email.

The challenge paper (arXiv 2601.05564) describes the full-duplex track as over 107 hours of real recordings in Chinese and English, more than 100 speakers (from a web search summary of the paper; not checked against the PDF).
